# ECONOMICS

# Product Economics Overview

SpendPilot AI is designed as a lightweight SaaS optimization platform with relatively low infrastructure costs and high potential operating margins.

The product primarily performs:
- form processing,
- rule-based audit calculations,
- report storage,
- and lightweight analytics rendering.

This makes the operational cost structure relatively efficient compared to compute-heavy AI applications.

---

# Cost Structure

## Frontend Hosting

Platform:
- Vercel

Estimated Cost:
- Free tier sufficient for MVP
- ~$20/month for small production workloads

---

## Backend Hosting

Platform:
- Render

Estimated Cost:
- Free tier sufficient for MVP
- ~$25–50/month for moderate production traffic

---

## Database

Platform:
- MongoDB Atlas

Estimated Cost:
- Free tier sufficient for MVP
- ~$20–100/month depending on storage and traffic

---

## AI Costs

Current Version:
- No direct LLM inference costs
- Rule-based recommendation engine

This significantly improves operating margins during early-stage growth.

Future AI-powered recommendation systems may introduce:
- OpenAI API costs
- Anthropic API costs
- vector database costs
- inference scaling costs

# Gross Margin Expectations

The platform is expected to maintain strong gross margins because:

- frontend hosting is inexpensive,
- audit calculations are lightweight,
- storage requirements are relatively small,
- and recommendation generation is computationally cheap.

Margins may decrease if:
- heavy LLM inference is introduced,
- or continuous monitoring infrastructure scales aggressively.

---

# Scalability Considerations

The architecture is relatively scalable because:

- the frontend is statically deployed,
- APIs are lightweight,
- and MongoDB scales horizontally.

Potential future scaling investments:
- Redis caching
- background job queues
- analytics pipelines
- vector search infrastructure

---

# Key Business Risk

The largest economic risk is user retention.

Many users may only need:
- one-time audits,
- or infrequent optimization checks.

To improve recurring revenue potential, future versions would likely require:
- continuous monitoring,
- recurring alerts,
- and organization-level collaboration features.

---

# Economic Philosophy

The product intentionally prioritizes:
- simplicity,
- explainability,
- and low operational overhead.

The current rule-based recommendation engine avoids expensive inference costs while still providing actionable optimization insights.

This makes the MVP economically efficient and easier to scale during early growth stages.
